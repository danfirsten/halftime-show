halftime show

Halftime show.

The design direction for Dan's design gate, 17 November 2026. An extension that mutes the ad break and puts something better on top. Every word, score and question on this page is a sample. The overlay is always ink; the page around it follows the theme switch above.

The moment

Clip open on the ad, clip shut on the show. Sped up: an 8 second break
Sample episodeSound on
  1. The show plays, sound on
  2. An ad starts. The tab mutes first
  3. The clip opens over the ad, 700 ms, and the inside settles, 900 ms
  4. The menu starts the last show used in 3
  5. The edge drains as the break runs
  6. The show is back. Sound on first, then the clip shuts, 480 ms
  7. The back note, 4 s

With reduced motion on: the mute and unmute are the same, the overlay fades in and out over 150 ms with no clip and no settle, and the edge clock steps every 5 seconds.

The four halftime shows

Click the player, then use the keys. 1 to 4 switch

Five letters: type a guess and press Enter (the sample answer is a word about breaks). Filled tile: right letter, right place. Outline with a ring: in the word, wrong place. Hoop's catch: dots are caught by falling through the ring; arrow keys or the pointer move Hoop, and it plays itself until you do. Something to look at finishes as the break does; here a break is 40 seconds.

Privacy

Halftime Show collects nothing and makes no network calls. Settings and scores stay in this browser. It doesn't block, skip or speed up ads: the ad still plays, muted and covered. Halftime Show is not made by or affiliated with Hulu.
