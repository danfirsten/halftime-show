# Font licences

Geist and Geist Mono are by Vercel, with Basement Studio, under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1. They are bundled from the Fontsource packages `@fontsource-variable/geist` and `@fontsource-variable/geist-mono`, whose `LICENSE` files carry the full text. Every build ships those texts in its `licences/` folder. Nothing is fetched at run time.
